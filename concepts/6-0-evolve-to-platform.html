Evolve from SaaS Product to Platform

From delivering data to customers, through packaging capabilities as distributable apps, to enabling collaborative analytics across organizational boundaries.

The Opportunity

A SaaS product serves users. A platform serves users and enables others to build on top of your data and capabilities. The distinction matters commercially: platforms create network effects, partner ecosystems, and expansion revenue that purely product-driven companies cannot access. Snowflake's distribution layer makes this transition concrete. The same data that you currently deliver to customers via Reader Accounts or Secure Shares can be packaged as a Native App with embedded AI, published to the Snowflake Marketplace so partners and enterprise customers can discover and install it, and opened to collaborative analysis via clean rooms where strategic customers contribute their own data. Each step on this journey adds a new monetization motion without requiring you to rebuild the underlying data infrastructure.

Storyboard

1

Start: Deliver Reports

Your starting point: customers receive live reports via Secure Share or Reader Account. Zero-copy, always current. This is effective but limited: you control the data, they control the analysis tool, and there is no packaging or monetization beyond the initial sale.

See 6.1 for the delivery model decision framework. Secure Sharing is the right foundation: it is zero-copy, always live, and scales to any customer with a Snowflake account without operational overhead.
2

Evolve: Package as an App

Package the data plus an embedded analytics layer and an AI agent into a Native App. Now customers install a product rather than receiving a share. You control the versioning, the UI, the AI capabilities, and the upgrade cycle.

See 6.2 for Native App packaging. The app bundles the semantic views from Section 3, the Cortex Agent from Section 4, and the analytics dashboard from Section 5 as a single installable artifact.
3

Distribute: Publish to Marketplace

Publish the Native App to the Snowflake Marketplace as a paid listing. Enterprise customers and Snowflake partners can discover, trial, and subscribe without a sales call. Your data product now has a distribution channel that scales independently of your sales team.

Marketplace listings support free, trial, and paid access models. Auto-fulfillment handles cross-region replication automatically. One app published once is available to all Snowflake customers in supported regions.
4

Collaborate: Open a Clean Room

For strategic enterprise accounts, open a clean room where the customer can contribute their own internal data to run collaborative analyses: overlap analysis, attribution modeling, enrichment. Both parties gain insight without either exposing raw records.

See 6.3 for the clean room pattern. This is the most advanced step on the platform journey: you are no longer just delivering data, you are enabling customers to combine your data with theirs to create insight that neither party could generate alone.
