Real-Time Product Intelligence

Turn your data pipeline into an intelligence layer that detects signals, triggers actions, and keeps your team ahead of what customers are experiencing.

The Opportunity

Most SaaS platforms collect data. Fewer act on it in real time. Once your product analytics and multi-source data flow into Snowflake, the infrastructure is already in place to do more than store and query. You can detect meaningful signals as they emerge: a tenant's engagement dropping, an anomaly in API usage, a cohort approaching a churn threshold. Combined with Snowflake's native task and alert capabilities, those signals can trigger automated actions across your platform without building a separate eventing system.

Storyboard

1

Events Flow In

Raw clickstream events, API calls, and feature usage land in Snowflake via Snowpipe Streaming. Every event carries a tenant identifier and timestamp. Nothing is batched.

See 2.1 for the ingestion pipeline. The key output: a live product_events table with sub-second latency from your application to Snowflake.
2

Identities Are Resolved

Events from different sources (your app, your CRM, your billing system) may refer to the same customer with different identifiers. Entity resolution maps them all to a canonical tenant_id.

See 2.2 for the identity resolution pipeline. The output: a canonical_entities table that bridges all source identifiers to a single customer record.
3

Signals Are Computed

A dynamic table continuously computes a rolling engagement score per tenant based on the last 7 days of activity. It updates every 5 minutes. No cron jobs, no schedulers.

A simple example: engagement_score = (active_days / 7) * log(api_calls + 1) computed as a dynamic table with TARGET_LAG = '5 minutes'. Scores below a threshold flag a tenant as at-risk.
4

Actions Are Triggered

A Snowflake Alert monitors the engagement score dynamic table. When a tenant drops below the at-risk threshold, a Snowflake Task calls an external webhook to notify your CS team or trigger an in-app nudge.

This replaces a Kafka consumer, Lambda function, and a separate alerting service with two native Snowflake objects: an Alert and a Task. The entire pipeline stays within Snowflake.
