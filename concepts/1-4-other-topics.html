Other Foundation & Operations Topics

Additional patterns for multi-tenant SaaS platforms that didn't warrant a full deep-dive page -- but are worth knowing about when building on Snowflake.

Cross-Tenant Data Sharing

Enabling your customers to share data with each other (or with you) using Snowflake's native secure sharing -- no ETL, no copies, instant access. Useful when tenants collaborate on shared datasets or when you need to aggregate anonymized data across tenants.
